Chapter 01

High Concept & Executive Summary

OBSOLETO is a contemplative narrative survival experience exploring routine, quiet dignity, and late redemption: the journey of an evicted salaryman living in his Toyota Probox station wagon, keeping his stove burning in the open trunk to fund a long-promised pilgrimage to Cape Sōya and typing out the novel his deceased wife never got to write.

🚗

The Salaryman's Wagon

A 1998 Toyota Probox. Heavy 60–80 km/h cruising physics: feel the vibrating thin sheet metal, worn windshield wipers, and coolant radiator hiss.

🍜

Trunk Noodle Micro-Stall

Tactile timing mini-games to pour boiling water, slice scallions with a pocket knife on the spare tire, and tune 100-yen ramen for night shift workers.

⌨️

Midnight Typewriter

Rhythmic typing mini-game on her mechanical machine (*clack-clack-ding*). Reliving honest, raw daily encounters to complete her legacy.

«You know it in your gut: a fresh graduate costs a third of your salary, navigates automated tools with ease, and doesn't have tired eyes. To corporate algorithms, you are simply obsolete overhead.»

— The Premise of OBSOLETO
Chapter 02

Narrative Synopsis & Character Arc

Set in contemporary Japan, you play as Kenji (47), an administrative clerk laid off during corporate restructuring after 25 years of faithful service. Unable to pay metropolitan rent and refusing to burden his adult children with debts, he pawns his smartphone for emergency groceries and packs what remains of his life into his modest white Probox.

📦 Packing: The 4 Memory Artifacts

In the cargo bed, only the fragments of a lifetime fit:

  • The Ceramic Urn & Portrait: His late wife's ashes wrapped in silk on the copilot seat, her photo taped beside the A/C vents.
  • Folded Paper Road Map: Bought at a highway gas station. No GPS or phone data; his only guide across national secondary routes (*kokudō*).
  • 35mm Film Camera: Bought 15 years ago for a road trip endlessly delayed. Exactly 24 frames left on the roll.
  • Her Mechanical Typewriter: She dreamed of being a novelist; he promised her quiet time that 25 years of unpaid overtime stole.

📞 The Green NTT Payphone Dilemma

At village rest stops, green NTT coin payphones hum in the dark. Inserting a hard-earned 100-yen coin, Kenji dials his son's number:

  • Tell the Truth: Confess he lost his job, his home, and lives in the wagon. Moral relief, but intensifies filial guilt.
  • Maintain the Lie: Choke back tears and say: «Everything is going great here, son. Just wanted to hear your voice». Preserves pride, deepens loneliness.
Developer's Note

Project Genesis: Silent Battles and Hot Broth

«Not all battles are fought with assault rifles or in bloody wars. Most of us fight invisible battles every single day: against the cold, against routine, and against the terror of being discarded.»

The inspiration for OBSOLETO didn't come from a boardroom meeting analyzing Steam retention curves. It came from real-life experiences, forgotten accounts, countless videos documenting quiet daily life on the fringes of Japan, and my own dreams. From watching time slip away relentlessly, watching dreams come and go, and realizing that to modern corporations, humans are often treated as nothing more than replaceable lever-pullers.

As an AI Architect, I face the paradox of our era every day: a colossal technological capability too often abused out of laziness to flood the world with hollow stories, soulless games, and flat characters devoid of human tropes or genuine pain. We live in a quiet generational clash: the young who look down on the old, convinced nothing before them held any value; and the old who refuse to adapt, convinced every novelty is a disaster. Yet beneath all that noise lies a latent terror that touches everyone: the fear that your time will run out, and the world will declare you obsolete.

This is a reality millions already endure: an ache of nostalgia, broken promises, and an endless story that never pauses. No matter how much you endure today, tomorrow you will have to endure a little more, regardless of the freezing rain, hunger, or bone-deep exhaustion.

Every day, we are all fighting for the same thing—survival and purpose—but some people carry far heavier battles. And those battles aren't fought with gunpowder: they are fought silently in the front seat of a beat-up car on a frozen roadside. And sometimes, in the middle of that storm, a simple bowl of hot broth is all a human being needs to smile, catch their breath, and keep going for one more kilometer.

// THE TOYOTA PROBOX FACTOR

In Japan, the white Probox is the quintessential symbol of the unglamorous working salaryman. Zero vanity: rear seats fold completely flat to hold cardboard sample boxes, the dashboard has an integrated pull-out table to eat noodles on the go, and the 1NZ-FE engine refuses to die. It is the only true vehicle for someone with nothing left but the highway.

// THE HONEST AUDIENCE

This game is not designed for genre purists; it is for the honest ones. For those who know the weight of unreciprocated loyalty, the quiet sting of a phone call you were too proud to make, and the solace of modest warmth at the end of an exhausting shift.

Chapter 03

Core Design Pillars

Pillar 01

Dignity of Routine

Victory isn't building a fast-food empire. It’s earning enough coins for 15 liters of regular gasoline, replacing a blown radiator hose, and having 30 quiet midnight minutes to write.

Pillar 02

The Tripartite Loop

A continuous 24-hour cycle alternating between meditative road transit, tactile rapid noodle cooking at dusk, and intimate midnight typewriter reflection.

Pillar 03

Pure Diegesis

Zero floating mini-maps or intrusive digital HUDs. Fuel is read on the dashboard needle; engine health in radiator steam; money in the cup holder coins; route navigation on the steering wheel's paper map.

Chapter 06

The Trunk Noodle Stall (The Midnight Hearth)

At night, the tailgate opens to reveal a portable gas stove, boiling kettle, and ramen cups.

🫖

Boiling Water QTE

Hold trigger to pour boiling water precisely to the inner line. Under/over-filling spoils the noodle texture and reduces payment.

🔪

Bumper Cutting Board

Slice scallions with a pocket knife on the spare tire board; crack a raw egg to poach in residual broth heat.

🪙

Physical Coin Tips

Higher broth satisfaction yields extra 100-yen coins, unlocking customer confessions and fuel funds.

Chapter 07

Her Mechanical Typewriter: Raw Truth Without Embellishment

Kenji is not a fiction author. He simply recounts the raw, unembellished truth of his day: the cold steering wheel, the sizzle of the gas burner, three coins left by a trucker, his aching back, and the hollow silence after hanging up the NTT payphone.

FIG 03 // ARTIFACTS OF MEMORY — TYPEWRITER, NTT PAYPHONE & 35MM CAMERA MECHANICAL RELICS
Typewriter Mechanism 1980s Japanese portable mechanical typewriter
Diegetic Audio Lead clacks, bell *ding*, and manual carriage return
Analog Camera 24 exposures developed during the end credits album
Calling the Son 100¥ coins at green NTT highway payphones

Diegetic Typing Rhythm Mechanic

  • Key phrases from customer encounters appear as rhythmic beats across the paper sheet.
  • Pressing keys to the beat produces the tactile strike of lead against the platen roller (*clack-clack-clack*) alongside ambient piano music.
  • Hitting the right margin rings the bell (*ding!*); push carriage return (*shhh-clack*) to feed a new line.
  • Repeated timing errors tangle the typebars; manually unjam them before continuing.
Chapter 08

Spatial Inventory Management (Probox Trunk Tetris)

The rear floor folds 100% flat, but physical volume remains strictly limited:

Rigid Grid Constraints

  • Sleeping bag occupies 6 permanent slots.
  • Ceramic urn & framed portrait are fixed on the copilot seat.
  • 24-cup noodle cartons take 4 slots each.
  • Spare 5L gas can and butane tank compete for ventilated floor corners.

The Passenger Trade-off

Picking up an elderly hitchhiker or stranded driver means shifting trunk cartons to the floorboards to clear the rear bench, sacrificing space for extra emergency supplies.

Chapter 10

Game Modes & Cape Sōya Climax

1. The Pilgrimage

Main Campaign (6–8 hours). Full voyage from Tokyo outskirts to Cape Sōya. Balanced economic and vehicle survival.

2. Short Route

Bite-sized story (2 hours). Contained 2-region route ideal for direct, focused sessions.

3. Endless Zen Mode

Unlocked after campaign. Procedural rainy secondary roads with no end date, tailored for streaming and relaxation.

«At Cape Sōya, the road ends where gray northern waves break against the sea wall. Kenji sets his stove on the hood and prepares two steaming noodle cups: one for himself, and one set beside her ceramic urn. Inside the Probox, illuminated by battery light, he types the final pages of her manuscript. At sunrise, he scatters her ashes to the polar wind. Both promises have been fulfilled.»

— The Ending of OBSOLETO
Chapter 11

Art Style & Sound Identity

🎨 Stylized Low-Poly Retro (32-Bit)

Minimalist geometry elevated by atmospheric volumetric lighting: halogen headlight beams slicing through dense fog, amber lantern glow, and rain shaders on glass.

🎧 Diegetic Foley Audio (50% of Immersion)

Raindrops pelting thin roof sheet metal, worn windshield wipers squeaking, gas flame hiss, typewriter clack-ding, and subtle lo-fi acoustic folk on tape.

Chapter 12

Technical Feasibility for Solo Dev

Typical Production Bottleneck Pragmatic Solution in OBSOLETO
Modeling hundreds of kilometers of open world Node-Graph + ~20 Modular Highway Slices (curves, tunnels, bridges) procedurally stitched.
Expensive voice acting & cinematic motion capture Environmental storytelling & diegetic text. Foley sound design conveys emotion without voice talent.
Complex 3D character rigs Stylized low-poly silhouettes with minimal animation and 2D illustrated dialogue portraits.
Chapter 13

Prototyping Roadmap (4 Phases / 16 Weeks)

Weeks 1 to 4

Phase 1: Cockpit & Heavy Inertia

Basic car controller with heavy weight physics and fixed cockpit camera. 3 modular road slices with volumetric fog and rain.

Weeks 5 to 8

Phase 2: The Trunk Noodle Stall

Fixed tailgate camera. Working butane stove, boiling pour QTE, scallion cutting, cup holder coin counter, and first test customer.

Weeks 9 to 12

Phase 3: The Typewriter & Map

Diegetic rhythmic typewriter with key jam mechanic. 2D paper highway map with node selection and route tracking.

Weeks 13 to 16

Phase 4: Vertical Slice (Playable Demo)

Integrate all three layers into a continuous 24h daily loop (1 village, 1 highway transit, 1 night stall, 1 typing session). Ready for Steam / itch.io demo.
